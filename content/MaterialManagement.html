Material Management

Relationship/Prerequisites

  • Material data defines how products, parts, and build requirements relate to each other.
  • Keep foundational records accurate before using them in sales, build, inventory, or service workflows.

SKUs

  • SKUs identify the sellable products and parts used by the system.
  • They support ordering, inventory tracking, and workflow reporting.

PAMs

  • PAM records connect products to the materials required to build or service them.
  • These relationships help the system calculate demand and identify shortages.
