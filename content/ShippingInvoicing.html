Shipping & Invoicing

Shipping

  • Shipping work begins after the service or build activity is resolved.
  • Enter shipment notes on the ticket so the rest of the team can see current status.
  • Mark the shipping step complete once the item has been packaged and placed in the shipping queue.

Invoicing

  • Invoice preparation uses ticket details, parts used, time logged, and customer billing data.
  • Enter an invoice note when the invoice has been sent.
  • When both shipping and invoicing are complete, the related ticket can close.
