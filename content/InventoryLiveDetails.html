Live Inventory Details

Purpose

This page expands on the Live Inventory dashboard while the main Inventory page stays concise and consistent with the rest of the guide.

How to Read the Dashboard

Use these fields as your operating baseline when reviewing current and near-term supply status.

  • On Hand: Physical quantity currently in stock.
  • Reserved: Quantity already allocated to active service tickets and production builds.
  • Available: Quantity that can be used now without impacting committed work.
  • Projected Need: Forecasted reorder demand based on current shortages and upcoming order commitments.

Click dashboard metrics to drill into the activity that generated each value and verify whether shortages are caused by incoming demand, active consumption, or delayed replenishment.

If Available drops unexpectedly while On Hand appears stable, verify new reservations from Sales Orders or open Service Tickets before manually adjusting quantities.
